Questions people ask before paying

Is this allowed on X?

Yes. Publishing runs through X's official API with scoped permissions, every post is approved by a human (you), and nothing unsolicited ever goes out. That combination is exactly what the platform rules permit. Reference case: my own account has run this way in public since day one.

Why is the setup split into CHF 330 + 660?

So you see it working before most of the money moves. CHF 330 starts the build. The remaining CHF 660 is charged automatically — to the card you saved at checkout — only once your first three posts are live on your account. If we never get you to three live posts, the 660 never charges.

Will it sound like me or like AI?

The system writes inside your voice document — your topics, your sentence rhythm, your banned words. A quality gate rejects generic drafts before you see them. And you are the final filter: anything that doesn't sound like you gets a FALSE and never publishes.

How much of my time does it really take?

One approval pass per day, typically 5 minutes from your phone: read the drafts, flip TRUE or FALSE. Skip a day and the queue simply waits — approved posts keep publishing from your buffer.

What happens if I cancel?

Monthly ops cancel anytime. The system, the sheet, and the documentation are set up on assets you can keep — you're paying me to build and run it, not to rent your own account back.
