How it will work

Same shape as X Autopilot, which already runs this way in public. Video adds one stage — production — and changes nothing else.

Step 1

Channel voice

Your subject, your format, your hard rules, references that sound and look like you. That becomes the document the system writes inside. It does not write outside it.

Step 2

System build

Topic mining, scripting, a quality gate, production and scheduled publishing, wired together and connected to your channel through official platform APIs.

Step 3

Your approval pass

Finished episodes queue up for you. Watch, flip TRUE or FALSE. Approved ones publish on your schedule. Skip a day and the queue simply waits.

Every day after

It just runs

New episodes on the schedule you set, a stats email on what went out, and an alert the moment anything stalls.
